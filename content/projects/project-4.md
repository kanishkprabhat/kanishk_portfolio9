<blockquote class="my-8 text-sm p-4 bg-gray-800/50 rounded-xl border border-gray-700 text-gray-400 italic">**Important:** This is a simulated campaign created to demonstrate my approach to YouTube lead generation. No live campaign performance or conversion results are being claimed.</blockquote>

The objective of this project was to develop a YouTube lead-generation campaign for a digital marketing training institute. Rather than treating YouTube simply as a video-awareness channel, I structured the campaign around a **lead-generation objective**, with the intended conversion being a prospective student's enquiry or demo-class booking.

## Pre-Launch & Improvements

During the review stage, two issues were identified that would need to be addressed before treating the campaign as launch-ready:

1. **Conversion Tracking:** Ensure conversion actions are verified before launch so the campaign can optimize against reliable lead signals.
2. **Budget Limitations:** Flagging the ■1,000/day budget as potentially restrictive for the configuration.

<div class="p-6 bg-blue-500/10 border border-blue-500/20 rounded-xl my-6"><h4 class="text-sm font-bold text-blue-400 mb-2 !mt-0">Landing Page Enhancements</h4><p class="text-sm text-blue-100/80 !m-0 leading-relaxed">Before spending full budget, I would test mobile load speed, clarity of proposition, visibility of the CTA, trust signals, and consistency between the video promise and landing-page message.</p></div>

<div class="flex items-center gap-3 font-mono text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-widest text-emerald-400 mb-2 mt-16"><span>19 / Key Learnings</span></div>

## Learnings & Project Outcome

<blockquote class="my-8 text-lg p-6 bg-gray-900 rounded-r-xl border-l-4 border-emerald-500 text-gray-300 italic">"The most important takeaway for me was that the creative and targeting strategy need to work together. A career-focused audience may respond differently to a 'job opportunity' hook than a business owner might respond to a 'grow your business' message."</blockquote>

**What this deliverable demonstrates:**
- Planning a YouTube lead-generation campaign.
- Researching and structuring audiences.
- Translating audience intent into targeting signals.
- Building a Google Ads video campaign and ad messaging.
- Creating a complete 30-second video concept.
- Structuring a conversion funnel and identifying pre-launch tracking issues.
